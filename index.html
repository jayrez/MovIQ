    How to play

    Identify the movie from its badly explained plot. You have six guesses. Type the full title or skip the hint letters with the on-screen or physical keyboard. Hint letters are locked. Use Backspace to erase and Enter to submit. Spaces and punctuation are filled automatically.

    Green means the letter is in the right spot. Yellow means it belongs elsewhere in that same word. Gray means there are no remaining matches in that word.
